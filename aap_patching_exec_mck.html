Why Automate Patching?

✕
Manual Patching Challenges
  • –Human error during patch steps
  • –No pre-patch snapshots or rollback plan
  • –Inconsistent process across servers
  • –No audit trail or patch history
  • –Services stopped/started as wrong user
  • –No verification after patching
  • –Time-consuming manual reporting
✓
AAP Automation Benefits
  • ▶Consistent repeatable process every time
  • ▶Auto Azure snapshot before patching
  • ▶Correct user for Tomcat stop/start
  • ▶Screenshot verification before & after
  • ▶Full patch history tracked in DB
  • ▶Automated email report on completion
  • ▶Zero manual steps — fully hands-off
